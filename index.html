You can't script everything. Including a career.

  1. SchoolThe freeze. A shy student walks on stage for a quiz competition, and nothing comes out.
  2. 1997The way back. Acting gets him on stage again, in a supporting role in Indera Bangsawan at Victoria Theatre.
  3. 2007On screen. He plays Shamsul in 2 x 5 Dol on Mediacorp Suria, one of dozens of television roles to come.
  4. 2011The realisation. Actors practise what people need at work every day. He founds The Speaking Factory on that idea.
  5. 2013The antidote. The boy who froze publishes The Stage Fright Antidote, and trains in improvisation at The Second City in Toronto.
  6. 2025Still on stage. He plays Encik Salim in Salina at Esplanade's Pesta Raya, with the same theatre company that gave him his start.
  7. NextThe next scene. A new book on how to say something when you don't know what to say.
